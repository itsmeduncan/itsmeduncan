## Duncan Grazier

Founder & CEO, [Whelk](https://getwhelk.com). Your hardware. Your models.

**Whelk** is private AI and agents. Native apps on iPhone, iPad, and Mac point at models you control, on-device or on your own server. Android, Linux, and the terminal are in testing. It is open core, private by default, and secure by design: no Whelk servers, no account, no telemetry. It scales from one phone to a whole team on one server, and it is free for personal use. Enterprise is coming soon.

Previously Chief AI Officer & CTO at [BuildOps](https://buildops.com) ($1B unicorn). Scaled Weedmaps from 30 to 300+ engineers through its public listing, then served as its CTO. VP of Engineering at ShopKeep, which sold to Lightspeed for $440M after my time there.
